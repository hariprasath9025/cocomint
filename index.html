Pure Intent.

Pollachi is celebrated as South India’s coconut sanctuary. Blessed by perennial rains from the Anamalai Hills and caressed by gentle winds, our coconut palms produce copra with uniquely dense fatty acids and an incomparably sweet, clean fragrance.

At COCOmint, we bridge the gap between this generational agro-heritage and your home. We refuse industrial solvents, high-temperature expellers, or chemical deodorizers. Each bottle is slow-pressed with patience and uncompromised purity.

Generational Craft

The Sacred Art of Mara Chekku

Modern industrial mills use extreme heat and chemicals to extract more oil. We deliberately stay slow.

01

Sun-Drying Copra

Only fully matured coconuts from Pollachi farms are selected, naturally split, and sun-dried until moisture is precisely below 6%.

02

Vaagai Wood Churn

Crushed gently in traditional wooden mortars carved from medicinal Vaagai (Siris) wood. Temperature never exceeds ambient room temperature.

03

Sediment Settling

Zero chemical refining or bleaching. The raw oil is left to settle naturally in food-grade stainless tanks over 48 hours.

04

Bottled Fresh

Poured into light-protected containers and sealed hermetically to lock in the heavenly natural aroma of virgin coconut straight to your kitchen.

❄️

The True Test of Purity: Natural Solidification

Unadulterated coconut oil naturally freezes below 24°C into a silky white butter. Commercial adulterated oils with liquid paraffin won’t solidify. COCOmint solidifies proudly — simply place the bottle in lukewarm water to liquefy.
