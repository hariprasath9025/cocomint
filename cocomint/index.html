Pure Intent.

Pollachi is celebrated as South India’s coconut sanctuary. Blessed by perennial rains from the Anamalai Hills and caressed by gentle winds, our coconut palms produce copra with uniquely dense fatty acids and an incomparably sweet, clean fragrance.

At COCOmint, we bridge the gap between this generational agro-heritage and your home. We refuse industrial solvents, high-temperature expellers, or chemical deodorizers. Each bottle is slow-pressed with patience and uncompromised purity.

Generational Farm-to-Bottle Craft

The Sacred Journey: Coconut to Pure Oil

Modern industrial mills use chemical solvents and scorching 200°C heat. We deliberately stay slow — honoring the centuries-old Vaagai wood cold-pressing tradition of Pollachi.

01
Pollachi Groves

Sun-Drying Copra

Fully matured coconuts from Pollachi farms are split by hand and naturally sun-dried on clean mats until moisture drops precisely below 6%.

Zero artificial heat 100% Sun-Cured
02
Cold-Pressed

Vaagai Wood Churn

Copra is crushed gently in ancient mortars carved from medicinal Vaagai (Siris) wood. Slow churn rotation keeps oil temperature strictly below 35°C.

Room Temperature Retains Lauric Acid
03
48-Hour Rest

Natural Settling

Zero chemical bleaching or chemical clay. Raw oil naturally settles in stainless decanters for 48 hours, followed by pristine organic cotton mesh filtration.

Zero Chemicals Natural Cotton Mesh

The Hallmark of Authenticity

The True Test of Purity: Natural Solidification Below 24°C

Unadulterated coconut oil naturally solidifies below 24°C into a silky, snow-white butter. Commercial adulterated oils blended with liquid paraffin remain artificially liquid. COCOmint solidifies proudly — simply place the bottle in lukewarm water for two minutes to liquefy.
